Why local garlic

Not the garlic you find at the grocery store.

Much of the garlic sold in stores is imported from overseas. Ours is grown a few minutes down the road.

  • 1

    Bigger cloves, easy to peel

    Siberian Hardneck grows a handful of large cloves per bulb. Less fiddling, more garlic.

  • 2

    Rich, full flavour

    Bold and warm when fresh, and it mellows nicely the longer it’s stored.

  • 3

    Grown for our winters

    A cold-hardy variety, planted by hand in Alberta soil every fall.
