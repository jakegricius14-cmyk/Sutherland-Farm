Questions

Good to know.

When will the 2027 garlic be ready?

Garlic is harvested in summer and then needs a few weeks to cure. We expect to start contacting the waitlist in late summer 2027.

Does it cost anything to join the waitlist?

No. Joining is free and you’re not committing to buy. It simply saves your place in line.

Where do you deliver?

Carstairs and Airdrie. If you live somewhere else nearby, call or text us and we’ll see what we can do.

How much garlic is in a pound?

It depends on the size of that year’s bulbs, but a pound is usually several large bulbs: plenty for a few weeks of regular cooking.

Can I plant your garlic?

Yes. Plant single cloves in the fall, pointy end up. See The Garlic page for simple steps.

I don’t use the internet much. Can I just call?

Of course. Call or text 403-899-8484 and we’ll add you to the list ourselves.
