Our story

A family farm
since 1919.

Sutherland Farm has been part of the Carstairs community for more than a century. Today, we put that land to work growing garlic, one clove at a time.

How we grow

Small rows, done by hand.

Every fall we separate the best bulbs into cloves and plant each one by hand, row after row, marked with wooden stakes so nothing gets lost under the snow.

Garlic is a slow crop. It spends the winter in the ground, wakes up in spring, and is pulled by hand the following summer. Then it hangs to cure for several weeks before it’s ready to sell.

We keep things small on purpose. It means we can look after every row ourselves, and you get garlic that was grown with real care.
